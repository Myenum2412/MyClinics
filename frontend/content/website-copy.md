## 8. FAQ

1. **Do I need an account to book?**
   Yes  a patient account keeps your appointments and records in one place. You can sign in with Google.
2. **Can I book through WhatsApp?**
   Yes. Message the clinic's WhatsApp assistant, tell it which doctor or department you need, and it will book your appointment.
3. **Is my health data safe?**
   Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and only the clinic's care team can access your records. We never sell your data.
4. **What if I need to cancel or reschedule?**
   Cancel or reschedule from your account, by phone, or through the WhatsApp assistant  anytime.
5. **Which departments and doctors are available?**
   Each clinic sets its own doctors and departments. Once you pick a clinic, you'll see its full list with available slots.
6. **Can I see my past prescriptions and reports?**
   Yes  your account keeps your medical history, prescriptions, uploaded reports and bills.
7. **Does the clinic need technical staff to set this up?**
   No. Setup is filling in a few forms: clinic name, doctors, services and opening hours.
8. **Can multiple staff use the same account?**
   Staff each get their own secure login. Access follows roles, so everyone sees only what they need.
9. **Is my phone number used for anything else?**
   Only for appointment confirmations and reminders. You can opt out at any time.
10. **What happens to my data if I leave the clinic?**
   Records are kept only as long as the clinic needs them for your care and the law requires, then securely deleted.

## 11. Notes for Implementation

- **Tone:** professional, warm, trustworthy  healthcare, not hype. No exclamation marks, no invented stats.
- **Both audiences in one hero:** the headline speaks to everyone; the subheadline splits the two paths (book / manage) and the page leads with patients, then the clinic pitch, then a shared how-it-works.
- **CTA naming:** "Book an Appointment" (patient) and "Get Started" (clinic)  both outcome-focused; avoid "Learn More" / "Submit".
- **Accessibility:** keep all links as real `<a href>`/`<Link>` tags; the current nav already follows this pattern.

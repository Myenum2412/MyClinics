## 🎯 Campaign Strategy & Reel Production Guidelines

### Reel Structure Framework (The 60-Second Conversion Engine)
* **00s - 05s | The Hook:** High visual agitation, sharp sound effect, calls out specific clinic pain.
* **05s - 20s | The Agitation:** Relatable everyday clinic reality (ringing phones, lost revenue, shouting names).
* **20s - 40s | The Solution (MyClinics Demo):** Visual showcase of WhatsApp bot + clean web dashboard.
* **40s - 50s | The Transformation / ROI:** Concrete numbers (e.g. 60% fewer no-shows, 3 hours daily saved).
* **50s - 60s | The Sales Conversion CTA:** Direct instruction (Comment 'CLINIC', DM 'DEMO', link in bio for 14-day free trial).

### Production Best Practices
1. **Pacing:** 135–150 words per 60 seconds. Keep the delivery punchy, confident, and empathetic.
2. **Visuals:** Split screen showing **Old Way (Stress)** vs **MyClinics Way (Calm & Automated)**.
3. **Captions:** Dynamic kinetic text with highlighting (e.g., Red for Pain, Green for Revenue/Savings).

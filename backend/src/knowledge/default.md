## Location

The clinic is located at 42 Green Park Road, MG Road, Kochi, Kerala 682016.
The entrance is on Green Park Road, next to the Green Park pharmacy.
Google Maps link: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=42+Green+Park+Road%2C+MG+Road%2C+Kochi%2C+Kerala+682016

## Opening hours

The clinic is open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
The clinic is closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

## Consultation fees

A standard consultation fee is Rs. 500 per visit.
Follow-up consultations within 15 days of a previous visit cost Rs. 300.
Telephone and video consultations cost Rs. 400.

## Payments and insurance

We accept cash, UPI, and all major credit and debit cards.
We do not process insurance claims directly, but we provide a detailed receipt
and invoice that you can submit to your insurer.

## Contact information

To reach the clinic, call or WhatsApp 0484 220 1234 during opening hours.
For emergencies outside opening hours, please call 112 or go to the nearest
hospital emergency department.

## Appointments policy

Appointments are required for all doctor consultations. Walk-ins are accepted
only when a doctor has an open slot. Please arrive 10 minutes before your
appointment time.
